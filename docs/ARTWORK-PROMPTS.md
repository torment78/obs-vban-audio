# VBAN Stream artwork editing prompts

Edited with the built-in imagegen tool. Each image is a separate text-only edit of the corresponding existing banner. PNG masters and JPEG exports are in `docs/images`.

## wide-tagged

```text
Use case: text-localization.
Asset type: wide horizontal product banner.
Input image: the sole attached image is the exact edit target, not a style reference.
Primary request: Rename this product from VBAN Audio to VBAN Stream. Change ONLY the large white word "AUDIO" to "STREAM" (S-T-R-E-A-M) immediately beneath "VBAN". Keep the same bold white type style, height and alignment; allow the new word to be wider to fit the extra letter cleanly within the existing title area.
Strict invariants: keep the exact existing canvas dimensions and aspect ratio, crop, layout, cyan arrow-ring waveform logo, two amber nodes, electronic lines, lighting, colors, background and every other word and credit unchanged. The large title must read "VBAN" on its first line and "STREAM" on its second line. Keep "8 inputs · 2 monitor returns", "Windows x64 | OBS Studio", and "by ElkaSoft" exactly as shown. Keep the small bottom-right VOICE MEETER badge and the caption 'VBAN protocol by VB-Audio' exactly as shown.
No redesign, no new elements, no movement of the logo or other text, no additional tagline. Output just this one edited banner at full quality.
```
