# Banner editing prompts

These final variants were created with the built-in imagegen tool. The original
OBS banner supplied the foreground brand; VBAN Plug supplied the depth and layout
reference. The files below are the selected final variants.

## Horizontal with credit

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit.
Asset: finished wide OBS VBAN Audio README / website / social banner, landscape 2:1.
IMAGE 1 is the original wide OBS VBAN AUDIO banner and is the EDIT TARGET. IMAGE 2 is the tall VBAN PLUG VST banner and is ONLY a reference for background depth, restrained luminous finish, footer credits and brand family. This is NOT a VBAN PLUG graphic. Direct file references are unavailable, so use the displayed inputs.
Critical clarification from user: the OBS electronic background lines MUST REMAIN STRAIGHT, with crisp angled bends like circuit traces. Borrow depth and glow from the VST reference, NOT its flowing waves. No curved strands, no sine waves, no flowing ribbons anywhere in the background.

Keep the original OBS icon on the LEFT unchanged in design and scale: same exact cyan two-arrow ring, same cyan multi-bar waveform, same two amber endpoint circles with cyan rims, same colors and proportions. Do not import or redraw the different VST icon. Keep the main white OBS VBAN / AUDIO title on the right and its exact font. Keep every existing word and its color.
Upgrade the dark navy BACKGROUND with layered straight blue/cyan electronic traces, restrained occasional amber light nodes, subtle atmospheric blue lighting, convincing near/far separation, soft glows beneath crisp foreground traces and dim softly defocused distant straight traces. Let these fine angular lines live mainly at the sides and lower third. The title and subtitle must be over a quiet dark area. Dim circuit lines should appear to recede in layered depth behind the foreground; rich and polished, still legible and restrained. NOT a busy circuit motherboard photograph. Background geometry is straight segments with 45-degree/90-degree bends ONLY.
Text wording verbatim, all present once:
"OBS VBAN"
"AUDIO"
"8 inputs · 2 monitor returns"
"Windows x64 | OBS Studio"
"by ElkaSoft"
Keep main title/subtitle and platform line approximately as on the target. A small layout refinement is allowed: move "by ElkaSoft" to the bottom-left footer with comfortable margins to match the reference family; "by" muted grey, "Elka" amber-orange, "Soft" cyan-blue, capital E and S, current italic styling.
Add the reference's small bottom-right credit: red rectangle with white "VOICE", adjacent white "MEETER", beneath it small grey text exactly "VBAN protocol by VB-Audio". Supporting attribution, not a headline. At least 30px safety margin.
No other text. No change to actual icon/logo artwork. No wavy or curving background lines. No mockup. High fidelity original branding with a deeper background.
```

## Vertical with credit

```text
Use case: compositing / layout adaptation.
Create a VERTICAL PORTRAIT companion to the most recent horizontal OBS VBAN AUDIO banner.
Input image 1 (tall VBAN PLUG) is only a vertical layout reference. Input image 2 (wide OBS VBAN AUDIO with straight circuit traces, ElkaSoft bottom-left and VOICE MEETER bottom-right) is the EXACT BRAND AND ARTWORK TARGET. The user has approved this newest straight-circuit look. Do not introduce waves from image 1.
Output a finished tall approximately 9:16 portrait banner, around 1024 by 1820 pixels. Adapt composition, not branding:
- Upper part: large centered bold white title on two lines exactly "OBS VBAN" then "AUDIO", same font/design as target.
- Center: the EXACT OBS target logo, unchanged artwork. Cyan two-arrow circular return ring, cyan multi-bar waveform, two amber endpoint nodes with cyan rims on its left and right. Preserve every icon element and its relative proportions; scale uniformly to fit. Never substitute the VBAN PLUG logo, never give the bottom arrow orange or purple coloring.
- Below icon, clear cyan subtitle exactly "8 inputs · 2 monitor returns". It can fit on one centered line at a readable smaller size.
- Below that, muted grey line exactly "Windows x64 | OBS Studio".
- Footer left: "by ElkaSoft", with muted grey italic by, orange Elka and cyan Soft, exact case.
- Footer right: small identical reference credit, red rectangle with white "VOICE", adjacent white "MEETER", small grey line underneath exactly "VBAN protocol by VB-Audio".
Same dark navy background, rich atmospheric depth, luminous blue/cyan electronic traces with occasional tiny amber nodes, near crisp traces and dim defocused distant traces. All traces must be STRAIGHT segments and 45/90-degree bends; NO curves, waves, flowing ribbons, or sine-wave strands. Circuit detail predominantly at the side edges and lower part. Maintain quiet dark negative space behind all text and logo. Harmonious vertical breathing room and generous safe margins, polished website/poster graphic. No added words, no mockup. This is OBS VBAN Audio by ElkaSoft, not VBAN Plug.
```
