VBAN Audio is ready.

Start OBS normally, then open Tools > VBAN Audio Settings.

For portable OBS, use your usual portable shortcut or launch option. This installer adds the plugin; it does not change how OBS itself starts.

The usage guide and uninstaller are in:
<OBS folder>\data\obs-plugins\obs-vban-audio

Standard installations can also be removed from Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > VBAN Audio.

Close OBS before updating or uninstalling. OBS scenes, profiles, and VBAN settings are preserved.
